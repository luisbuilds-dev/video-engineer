# Third-party software

Video Engineer is MIT-licensed. It depends on, but does not include, these projects:

- **Remotion** (https://www.remotion.dev) — free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people; larger companies need a Remotion company license. See https://www.remotion.dev/license.
- **whisper.cpp** (https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp, MIT) and its models, downloaded on your machine by `npm run instalar`.
- **ffmpeg** (https://ffmpeg.org, LGPL/GPL), installed by you.
- Fonts from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License), loaded through `@remotion/google-fonts`.
- **Ultra-Light-Fast-Generic-Face-Detector-1MB** (https://github.com/Linzaer/Ultra-Light-Fast-Generic-Face-Detector-1MB, MIT © 2019 linzai), model `modelos/ultraface-rfb-320.onnx` (commit dffddd, SHA-256 34cd7e60…f88017): finds the face so captions never cover it.
- **Background music** by Kevin MacLeod (https://incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0. Not included in this repo: `npm run musica -- --si` downloads it from the author's site. Credit the track in your post (`credito.txt`).
- **Sound effects** in `sonidos/sfx/` are original, generated by `herramientas/sintetizar-sfx.ts` (MIT, part of this repo).
- **Lucide icons** (https://lucide.dev, ISC), drawn and animated by the illustration scenes.
- **ONNX Runtime** (https://onnxruntime.ai, MIT), runs the face detector on your machine.

## Icons added from Iconify

Icons saved with `npm run iconos -- --guardar` keep the license of their collection (only MIT, ISC, Apache-2.0, CC0, BSD or CC BY 4.0 are accepted). Each one is listed with author and license in `iconos/CREDITOS.md`.

## OCR

[tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js) (Apache-2.0) with the Tesseract `spa` and `eng` language models (Apache-2.0), downloaded on first use to `models/tessdata/`.
